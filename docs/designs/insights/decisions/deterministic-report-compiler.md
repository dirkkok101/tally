## Description

Turn one coherent public evidence result into a complete bounded report without leaking orchestration or analytical invariants to callers.

## Decision

Use one concrete InsightReportCompiler to orchestrate CoherentEvidenceBinder, AnalysisWindowPolicy, ExactMetricCalculator, LinearPaceCalculator, EvidenceStateAccountant, InsightExplanationRenderer, InsightReportCanonicalizer, and InsightReportDiffer. The compiler accepts normalized request values, one frozen As-of Instant, one validated BudgetInsightEvidence result, and released policy versions. It emits a complete immutable InsightReport or one whole-report failure; it performs no storage or producer calls.

## Rationale

Per-metric public services would repeat evidence, time, ordering, provenance, and state-accounting rules. A monolithic handler would make exact policies difficult to test. One deep compiler with concrete pure collaborators gives a narrow boundary while retaining isolated arithmetic and property tests.

## Alternatives

A: one service per metric or signal exposed to callers, rejected because orchestration and reconciliation move outward. B: all calculation embedded in Generate, rejected because pure policy verification becomes indirect. C: one compiler over concrete pure collaborators, chosen.

## Consequences

Every requested kind is accounted for exactly once in released catalogue order. Signal-local unavailable states preserve exact metrics; coherence, compatibility, integrity, completeness, and exact-arithmetic failures produce no report. The canonical semantic payload excludes the derived reportId and contentFingerprint fields; its SHA-256 becomes contentFingerprint and deterministically derives reportId, avoiding a self-referential hash. No raw source member or merchant text is retained in output beyond bounded derived evidence and stable references.
