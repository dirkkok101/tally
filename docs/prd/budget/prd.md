## Problem Statement

Surface pain: trusted LEDGER actuals can show what was spent, but the owner still cannot tell whether monthly category spending aligns with intent because planned amounts live outside the same category and period model. 5 Whys: Why create monthly budgets? To know whether spending is on track before the month ends. Why are actuals insufficient? Actuals have no owner-approved target. Why can a spreadsheet or remembered amount not supply the target reliably? Its category identity, period boundary, revisions, and zero-versus-omitted meaning are detached from canonical LEDGER facts. Why does that matter downstream? BUDGET and INSIGHTS can otherwise select different periods or silently omit uncategorized and unbudgeted spend. Why does the mismatch persist? Tally has no authoritative, agent-operable plan contract that preserves monthly intent and composes with snapshot-consistent actuals. Root problem: Tally lacks a local, versioned monthly spending-plan baseline over stable LEDGER Spend Categories, so the owner must manually reconstruct intent and downstream on-track analysis cannot be reproducible. Without BUDGET, the owner continues comparing statements and remembered limits by hand, revisions lose provenance, and INSIGHTS has no trustworthy target. Journey: the sole Personal Finance Owner uses an AI Agent Host to discover BUDGET operations, creates or revises a monthly category plan, reviews exact totals and explicit omissions, activates it, and queries a snapshot-bound budget position while interpretation remains downstream.

## Personas

### P1: Personal Finance Owner

- **Role:** Sole owner and decision-maker for household finances
- **Goals:** When reviewing or planning personal spending, I want one trustworthy view across all owned accounts, evidence sources, spend pools, and categories so I can delegate maintenance safely and know why each transaction is present.
- **Pain Points:** Daily notifications and later statements can describe the same economic event; transactions are fragmented across three accounts at two banks; account, cardholder, payment instrument, pool, and category can be conflated; manual consolidation has weak provenance and uncertain duplicate handling.
- **Current Workaround:** Inspect separate notifications and statements, manually decide whether rows describe the same transaction, consolidate selected transactions, infer account or cardholder context, remove transfers or duplicates, assign categories and spending pools, and recalculate totals.
- **Success Looks Like:** The owner can delegate daily capture and monthly reconciliation, review every ambiguous or missing match, and retrieve exact pool/category totals whose evidence and decision history are inspectable.
- **Tech Level:** high
- **Usage Frequency:** daily

### UC-BUDGET-001: Draft a monthly category plan

- **Primary Actor:** P1
- **Trigger:** The owner prepares a plan for a current or future calendar-month Budget Period.
- **Depth Tier:** 3
- **Status:** draft

**Preconditions:**

The owner is authorized; LEDGER exposes a stable active Spend Category catalogue; the requested period and ZAR currency are explicit.

**Scenario:**

1. The owner selects an explicit calendar-month Budget Period. 2. BUDGET reads the applicable LEDGER Spend Category identities. 3. The owner submits independent non-negative exact ZAR Category Budget Entries, using explicit zero for Zero Budget and omission for Unbudgeted. 4. BUDGET validates period, currency, category lifecycle, uniqueness, and replay identity. 5. BUDGET computes the plan total. 6. BUDGET atomically saves an immutable Draft Budget Plan Revision without changing the active revision.

**Postconditions:**

One attributable immutable draft exists; no active plan changed; no LEDGER state changed; a replay returns the same result without duplication.

**Failure Paths:**

- Invalid period, currency, negative amount, duplicate category, or malformed input is rejected without persistence.
- An inactive, archived, unknown, or stale category catalogue reference is rejected with an actionable error.
- A conflicting replay key or concurrent mutation is rejected without creating a partial or duplicate draft.

### UC-BUDGET-002: Activate or revise a plan

- **Primary Actor:** P1
- **Trigger:** The owner explicitly approves a draft for a current or future Budget Period.
- **Depth Tier:** 3
- **Status:** draft

**Preconditions:**

The draft is readable and valid against current LEDGER category lifecycle state; the period is not closed; the owner explicitly requested activation.

**Scenario:**

1. BUDGET presents the draft revision, computed total, and any current active revision. 2. The owner explicitly confirms activation. 3. BUDGET revalidates the draft and category references. 4. BUDGET atomically marks the draft Active and the previous active revision, if any, Superseded. 5. BUDGET records owner, reason, time, period, and revision provenance. 6. BUDGET returns the single current Active Budget Plan Revision.

**Postconditions:**

Exactly one active revision exists for the period; prior revisions remain immutable and readable; no LEDGER state changed; replay cannot create multiple activations.

**Failure Paths:**

- A completed Closed Budget Period rejects activation without restatement.
- A stale, inactive, archived, or otherwise invalid category reference rejects the entire activation.
- Concurrent activation, storage failure, or conflicting replay leaves the former active revision unchanged and creates no partial lifecycle transition.

### FR-BUDGET-CATEGORY-LIFECYCLE: Honor Spend Category lifecycle

- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Preserve stable LEDGER Spend Category identity while rejecting invalid new or activated plan intent. Implements G2 and G5 through UC-BUDGET-001, UC-BUDGET-002, and UC-BUDGET-004.
- **Rationale:** Category rename, archive, and reactivation must not rewrite history or silently revive planned amounts.

3. Given a referenced Spend Category is archived, when historical plan state is read, then its entries remain readable and are marked with current inactive lifecycle evidence.
4. Given an unknown, inactive, or archived Spend Category, when a new draft entry targets it, then BUDGET rejects the complete draft before mutation.
5. Given a Draft references a category that became unknown, inactive, or archived after draft creation, when activation is requested, then BUDGET rejects the complete activation and preserves the former active state.
6. Given an archived Spend Category is reactivated, when a later draft targets its stable identifier, then the new entry may be accepted but no prior amount is copied, restored, or activated automatically.
7. Given only a category display name and no stable identifier, when draft creation is requested, then BUDGET rejects the request before mutation.

- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Return deterministic current state and immutable Draft, Active, and Superseded plan evidence for one period. Implements G1, G3, and G5 through UC-BUDGET-004.
- **Rationale:** The owner must reconstruct what was proposed and what governed a period without treating superseded intent as deleted.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given a period with revisions, when List Plan Revisions is requested, then all Draft, Active, and Superseded revisions are returned by creation timestamp ascending with stable revision identifier ascending as the tie-breaker.
2. Given an explicit revision identifier, when Get Plan Revision succeeds, then its exact entries, computed total, period, currency, status, actor, reason, creation timestamp, and activation or supersession provenance are returned.
3. Given a completed period, when plan state is requested, then Closed Budget Period is returned while all revision history remains readable.
4. Given a Spend Category was renamed, when an earlier revision is read, then its stable category identifier is unchanged and any current display name is supplemental rather than a rewrite of stored intent.
5. Given a Spend Category is archived, when an earlier revision is read, then its entry remains readable and is marked with the current inactive lifecycle state without becoming eligible for new use.
6. Given an unknown revision identifier, when Get Plan Revision is requested, then BUDGET returns a stable Not Found result and no plan state changes.

### FR-BUDGET-PLAN-IDENTITY: Identify monthly Budget Plans

- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Establish one stable Budget Plan identity for one explicit ZAR calendar-month period. Implements G1 and supports UC-BUDGET-001 and UC-BUDGET-004 under A1, C4, and C15.
- **Rationale:** Every draft, activation, history read, and position query needs the same non-inferred period identity.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given a valid year and month in the accepted ZAR boundary, when the first Create Draft for that period succeeds, then one Budget Plan identity is created whose period starts on that month first Effective Date inclusive and ends on the next month first Effective Date exclusive.
2. Given an existing Budget Plan and the same explicit year and month, when any later plan operation is requested, then the same stable plan identity, currency, start date, and end date are returned.
3. Given an operation whose schema requires a Budget Period and the caller omits it or relies only on invocation time, when the operation is requested, then BUDGET rejects the request before reading or mutating plan state.
4. Given a non-calendar interval, invalid month, or non-ZAR currency for an operation that accepts a Budget Period, when the operation is requested, then BUDGET returns a stable validation error and creates no plan or revision.
5. Given a valid period with no Budget Plan, when plan state is requested, then BUDGET returns the explicit No Budget Plan state rather than Not Found, an empty plan, or a zero plan.
6. Given the trusted current Effective Date is within a Budget Period start-inclusive and end-exclusive boundaries, when its lifecycle eligibility is evaluated, then the period is Current.
7. Given the trusted current Effective Date is before a Budget Period start, when its lifecycle eligibility is evaluated, then the period is Future.
8. Given the trusted current Effective Date is on or after a Budget Period end, when its lifecycle eligibility is evaluated, then the period is Closed.

### FR-BUDGET-POSITION-QUERY: Calculate exact Budget Positions

- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Compare one explicit Budget Plan Revision with current corrected public LEDGER Budget Actuals under one snapshot, without interpretation. Implements G4 and G7 through UC-BUDGET-003.
- **Rationale:** The owner and INSIGHTS need one reproducible raw answer for plan versus actual before any pace, forecast, or narrative is added.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given a Budget Period with one Active revision and no revision selector, when Get Budget Position is requested, then the result is bound to that Active revision and reports its Active lifecycle status.
2. Given an explicit readable Draft, Active, or Superseded revision identifier, when Get Budget Position is requested, then that revision is evaluated without activation or lifecycle mutation and its status is returned.
3. Given a period has no Active revision and no revision selector, when Get Budget Position is requested, then BUDGET returns the explicit No Active Budget Plan Revision state and does not fabricate a zero plan.
4. Given a selected plan and no matching LEDGER actuals, when the position is returned, then every planned value is preserved, actual totals are exact zero, and the state remains distinct from an empty or absent plan.
5. Given LEDGER actuals assigned to a category with a positive planned entry, when the position is calculated, then those actuals appear exactly once in that category budgeted position.
6. Given LEDGER actuals assigned to a category with a Zero Budget entry, when the position is calculated, then those actuals appear exactly once in that category Zero Budget Position.
7. Given LEDGER actuals assigned to a valid Spend Category omitted from the selected revision and no element of that Budget Actual's frozen Spend Category ancestry carries a Category Budget Entry, when the position is calculated, then those actuals appear exactly once as Unbudgeted Spend keyed by the exact assigned Spend Category identifier.
8. Given LEDGER actuals without a current Spend Category assignment, when the position is calculated, then those actuals appear exactly once as Uncategorized Spend and no category or planned amount is invented.
9. Given any selected plan and LEDGER snapshot, when category and total values are returned, then planned totals equal the exact sum of explicit entries and actual totals equal the exact sum of budgeted, Zero Budget, Unbudgeted Spend, and Uncategorized Spend actuals.
10. Given a planned category or total whose planned amount is at least its actual amount, when variance is returned, then Remaining equals planned minus actual and Over equals exact zero.
11. Given a planned category or total whose actual amount exceeds its planned amount, when variance is returned, then Over equals actual minus planned and Remaining equals exact zero.
12. Given a refund-heavy category or period produces a negative LEDGER Budget Actual, when the position is returned, then the negative value is preserved exactly and is not clamped or reclassified.
13. Given identical plan revision, LEDGER snapshot, period, category state, and categorization state, when the position is repeated, then categorized positions are ordered by stable category identifier ascending, the Uncategorized position follows them, and exact values, states, and provenance are semantically identical.
14. Given a successful position, when provenance is inspected, then it includes plan identity, revision identifier and status, period boundaries, currency, category-contract version, LEDGER contract version, snapshot identifier, and calculation schema version.
15. Given LEDGER corrections, allocation changes, relationship changes, voids, or supersessions after an earlier query, when a new position is requested, then current corrected actuals are recomputed under a new cited snapshot while the selected plan revision remains unchanged.
16. Given a position is returned, when its schema is inspected, then it contains no pace, forecast, trend, anomaly, recommendation, narrative, alert, or spending-control decision.
17. Given a position query completes, when durable BUDGET state is inspected, then no separate actual or report snapshot was persisted by BUDGET.
18. Given an Unbudgeted or Uncategorized position, when its values are returned, then Planned, Remaining, and Over are explicitly not applicable rather than zero, while its exact actual value remains present.
19. Given a valid period with no Budget Plan and no revision selector, when Get Budget Position is requested, then BUDGET returns the explicit No Budget Plan state and does not fabricate a plan, revision, or position.
20. Given a Category Budget Entry on a parent Spend Category and Budget Actuals assigned to that parent directly and to descendant Spend Categories one and two levels below that carry no entry, when the position is calculated, then every one of those Budget Actuals appears exactly once in the parent Budgeted position, and that row reports the direct subtotal, the descendant subtotal, and the absorbed descendant Spend Category identifiers in ascending ordinal order.
21. Given Category Budget Entries on both a parent Spend Category and one of its descendants, when the position is calculated, then a Budget Actual assigned at or below the descendant appears exactly once in the descendant position and is excluded from the parent position, while a Budget Actual under the parent outside the descendant subtree appears exactly once in the parent position.
22. Given a Category Budget Entry with Planned Spend equal to zero on a descendant Spend Category whose ancestor carries Planned Spend greater than zero, when the position is calculated, then Budget Actuals at or below that descendant appear exactly once in the descendant Zero Budget Position and no part of them falls back to the funded ancestor.
23. Given any selected revision and LEDGER snapshot, when every category position is returned, then each row's actual value equals the exact sum of its direct actual value and its descendant actual value, and the sum across all rows plus Uncategorized Spend equals the membership total, so no Budget Actual is counted more than once.
24. Given a Budget Plan Revision whose Spend Categories are all root nodes with no descendants, when the position is calculated, then every value, bucket, ordering, and total is identical to the `budget-position-v1` exact-identity result for the same inputs.
25. Given a successful position over a hierarchical Spend Category tree, when a Budget Actual member is inspected through the INSIGHTS read projection, then it reports its frozen Spend Category ancestry root-first with the assigned Spend Category last, and reports the effective Spend Category identifier that governed it, or null when the outcome is Unbudgeted Spend or Uncategorized Spend.
26. Given a Budget Actual member whose frozen Spend Category ancestry is empty for a categorized member, does not end with the assigned Spend Category identifier, repeats an identifier, or names an identifier absent from the known Spend Category evidence, when the position is calculated, then BUDGET returns the stable integrity failure and no partial totals.
27. Given Budget Actuals on two distinct Spend Categories whose shared ancestry carries no Category Budget Entry, when the position is calculated, then each produces its own Unbudgeted Spend row whose direct actual equals its row actual, whose descendant actual is exactly zero, and whose absorbed identifier list is empty, and no aggregated ancestor row is produced.

### FR-BUDGET-STRUCTURED-INVOCATION: Invoke BUDGET operations non-interactively

- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Provide deterministic JSON-oriented input, output, diagnostics, and failure behavior for every Public Budget Operation. Implements G6 through UC-BUDGET-005 under C1, C7, and C8.
- **Rationale:** Agents must distinguish business results from diagnostics and must never parse prose to determine whether plan intent changed.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given a Public Budget Operation and valid structured input through the documented non-interactive data path, when invocation succeeds, then exactly one structured business-result envelope is written to stdout and the process exits with the documented success code.
2. Given an expected validation, authority, conflict, lifecycle, compatibility, snapshot, resource, or Not Found failure, when invocation ends, then exactly one machine-readable BUDGET error envelope is written to stdout, diagnostics remain on stderr, and the documented non-zero exit category is stable.
3. Given malformed JSON or a schema-forbidden unknown field, when invocation is attempted, then validation fails before any plan read requiring financial output or any mutation.
4. Given any Public Budget Operation, when it is invoked, then it requires no interactive prompt, watcher, daemon, scheduler, network call, or background Tally process.
5. Given a successful or expected-failure invocation, when stderr, logs, telemetry, crash diagnostics, process arguments, and shared temporary locations are inspected, then they contain no category amount, plan entry, Budget Actual, position payload, credential, or serialized request or response body.
6. Given a mutation result whose delivery is interrupted, when the caller invokes the documented status or replay path, then the outcome is resolved through its idempotency key without heuristic inspection of storage.
7. Given an operation result or error, when a caller parses it, then operation name, contract version, result or error type, correlation identifier, and compatibility metadata are explicit.

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Active Budget Plan Revision** | The single explicitly activated Budget Plan Revision whose planned amounts govern the current Budget Position for its Budget Period. |
| **Budget Actual** | The exact transfer-, refund-, correction-, allocation-, void-, and supersession-aware aggregate returned by LEDGER for the requested snapshot and period. BUDGET does not reinterpret it as gross debits or total outflow. |
| **Budget Period** | One explicit monthly date interval for which the owner records spending intent; its boundaries and identity are stable and never inferred from invocation time. |
| **Budget Plan** | The stable owner planning identity for one Budget Period, containing a history of draft, active, and superseded Budget Plan Revisions. |
| **Budget Plan Revision** | An immutable version of a Budget Plan containing exact Category Budget Entries plus owner, reason, lifecycle, and creation provenance. |
| **Budget Position** | A deterministic snapshot-bound comparison of one explicit Budget Plan Revision with public LEDGER actuals. Without a revision selector it uses the single Active revision; an explicit Draft, Active, or Superseded revision may be evaluated without changing lifecycle state. The result returns exact planned, actual, remaining or over, Unbudgeted Spend, and Uncategorized Spend values plus revision status and provenance. |
| **Category Budget Entry** | An exact ZAR planned-spend amount for one stable LEDGER Spend Category identifier within one Budget Plan Revision. |
| **Closed Budget Period** | A completed calendar-month Budget Period that accepts no new Draft or activation in v1 while its existing plan revision history remains readable. Do not call it editable history. |
| **Draft Budget Plan Revision** | An immutable proposed Budget Plan Revision that has not been activated. Saving it does not change the Budget Position. Do not call it a working active budget. |
| **Planned Spend** | The exact ZAR amount in a selected Budget Plan Revision for a Category Budget Entry or the revision total. Its lifecycle status shows whether it is proposed Draft intent, owner-approved Active intent, or retained Superseded intent; it is never an actual or forecast. |
| **Public Budget Operation** | One versioned channel-neutral BUDGET capability using the canonical Create Draft, Get Plan Revision, List Plan Revisions, Activate Plan Revision, Get Budget Position, or Discover Contract vocabulary. It is not an HTTP endpoint or UI action. |
| **Superseded Budget Plan Revision** | A formerly active Budget Plan Revision replaced by an explicit activation and retained in history. Do not call it a deleted old budget. |
| **Unbudgeted Spend** | Actual spend assigned to a valid Spend Category for which the selected Budget Plan Revision has no Category Budget Entry. |
| **Uncategorized Spend** | Actual spend that LEDGER reports without a current Spend Category assignment; BUDGET exposes it separately and never treats it as zero or invents a plan category. |
| **Zero Budget** | An explicit Category Budget Entry of exactly ZAR 0.00, expressing that no spend is planned for the category; it is not the same as omission. |
