## Problem Statement

Surface pain: canonical transactions and exact monthly Budget Positions still leave Dirk manually deciding whether spending is on track, what changed, and which categories deserve attention. 5 Whys: Why are insights needed? Raw facts and plan totals require repeated interpretation. Why is repeated interpretation unreliable? Daily, weekly, and monthly comparisons use unstated windows, formulas, and correction timing. Why does BUDGET not answer this? BUDGET deliberately owns exact plan-versus-actual reconciliation and excludes pace, forecasts, trends, anomalies, recommendations, narratives, and retained reports. Why not ask an agent or spreadsheet ad hoc? The answer would be non-reproducible, could hide sparse evidence or corrections, and could not be compared with what was reported earlier. Root problem: Tally lacks a local, provenance-bound analytics layer that converts exact public BUDGET and LEDGER snapshots into reproducible periodic signals, explicitly distinguishes insufficient evidence, and preserves what the owner was shown without mutating financial truth. Without INSIGHTS, Tally remains a trustworthy record and planning tool but not timely decision support. Journey: the sole Personal Finance Owner, directly or through the AI Agent Host, discovers a versioned INSIGHTS operation, requests an explicit Budget Period, receives an explainable Insight Report bound to the trusted operation-start As-of Instant with exact metrics, a Linear Pace result, and explicit states for deferred or unsupported signal kinds, inspects prior Report Snapshots or a correction-based Restatement, and chooses any action outside INSIGHTS.

## Personas

### P1: Personal Finance Owner

- **Role:** Sole owner and decision-maker for household finances
- **Goals:** When reviewing or planning personal spending, I want one trustworthy view across all owned accounts, evidence sources, spend pools, and categories so I can delegate maintenance safely and know why each transaction is present.
- **Pain Points:** Daily notifications and later statements can describe the same economic event; transactions are fragmented across three accounts at two banks; account, cardholder, payment instrument, pool, and category can be conflated; manual consolidation has weak provenance and uncertain duplicate handling.
- **Current Workaround:** Inspect separate notifications and statements, manually decide whether rows describe the same transaction, consolidate selected transactions, infer account or cardholder context, remove transfers or duplicates, assign categories and spending pools, and recalculate totals.
- **Success Looks Like:** The owner can delegate daily capture and monthly reconciliation, review every ambiguous or missing match, and retrieve exact pool/category totals whose evidence and decision history are inspectable.
- **Tech Level:** high
- **Usage Frequency:** daily

**Scenario:**

### UC-INSIGHTS-004: Restate and compare a corrected report

- **Primary Actor:** P1

- **Depth Tier:** 3
- **Status:** draft

**Preconditions:**

The predecessor snapshot exists and passes integrity and compatibility checks; current compatible public producer evidence can be bound coherently; the request identifies permitted formula and threshold versions.

**Scenario:**

1. INSIGHTS loads but never modifies the predecessor. 2. It generates a candidate report through the same coherent on-demand workflow using current cited evidence and selected versions. 3. - Current evidence is unavailable or incoherent, so no partial restatement or diff is retained.

### UC-INSIGHTS-005: Manage retained history and recovery

- **Primary Actor:** P1
- **Trigger:** The owner explicitly requests report deletion, backup, verification, restore, or storage-status evidence.
- **Depth Tier:** 3
- **Status:** draft

**Preconditions:**

Tally is running inside the sole-owner local trust boundary and the requested operation uses compatible versions, bounded input, and safe owner-only paths where a target is required. Delete, Backup, and Restore requests include actor attribution and idempotency identity; Delete also names its reason and exact report target. Verify and Status are read-only within the invoking owner host account.

**Scenario:**

1. INSIGHTS previews the target retained identity and any surviving Restatement dependency. 2. Explicit deletion of a leaf validates actor, reason, idempotency identity, and permissions, atomically removes its report payload and link, and appends one metadata-only Deletion Event. 3. A referenced report is RESTRICTed until descendants are deleted from leaves to root; there is no automatic expiry or cleanup. 4. Backup captures all current durable INSIGHTS state with a manifest, fingerprint, version, permissions, and integrity evidence. 5. Verification independently checks completeness and integrity. 6. Restore builds and verifies a separate candidate, then atomically activates the exact selected backup state only after all checks pass. 7. Status returns metadata-only version, integrity, retained/deleted counts, and recovery evidence.

**Postconditions:**

Successful maintenance has one attributable complete outcome. Failure leaves the previously authoritative store and surviving report history unchanged. Existing backups remain point-in-time artifacts and may explicitly restore content deleted after they were created.

**Failure Paths:**

- Deletion without actor, reason, idempotency identity, or explicit target is rejected.
- Deletion of a Report Snapshot with a surviving Restatement dependency returns conflict and removes nothing.
- Unsafe path, symlink traversal, wrong ownership or permissions, incompatible version, corruption, omission, insufficient space, or failed integrity check blocks backup or restore activation.
- Cancellation or crash before activation leaves the prior store authoritative.
- Status and failures never emit report contents, amounts, merchant text, or private paths.

- **Primary Actor:** P1
- **Trigger:** The owner or AI Agent Host asks what INSIGHTS can do or invokes an INSIGHTS operation non-interactively.
- **Depth Tier:** 2
- **Status:** draft

**Preconditions:**

The local Tally executable is available inside the trusted host account.

**Scenario:**

1. The caller requests help, status, operation descriptors, or source-generated schemas without financial input. 2. INSIGHTS returns versioned operation names, mutability, authorities, schemas, limits, stable errors, exit categories, and compatibility ranges. 3. The caller sends structured input through stdin or an approved owner-only file. 4. INSIGHTS validates compatibility and boundaries, executes exactly one operation, emits exactly one structured business result on stdout, and confines metadata-only diagnostics to stderr.

**Postconditions:**

The caller can automate the operation without prompts, parsing prose, receiving hidden authority, or exposing financial values in process arguments.

**Failure Paths:**

- Unknown operation or invalid schema returns stable validation failure.
- Unsupported contract version returns compatibility failure before financial processing.
- Interactive prompt, extra stdout payload, payload-bearing stderr, network access, or hidden mutation authority is a contract failure.
- Cancellation or internal failure returns the documented exit category and no partial business result.

### FR-INSIGHTS-LINEAR-PACE: Calculate a transparent linear pace state

- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Compare month-to-date recorded actuals with an exact rational Linear Pace Baseline while exposing its uniform-spend limitation. Goal trace: G2.
- **Rationale:** A simple reproducible baseline can answer whether recorded spend is above or below a linear share without pretending to predict future behavior.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given a started Budget Period with a plan, when a Linear Pace Baseline is calculated, then elapsed calendar days include the As-of local date and total calendar days equal the complete Budget Period length.
2. Given a non-zero planned amount, when the baseline is calculated, then it equals planned minor units multiplied by elapsed days divided by total days as an exact rational value with no floating-point arithmetic.
3. Given exact recorded actual and the exact rational baseline, when pace is classified, then actual greater than the full planned amount returns Over Plan; otherwise exact comparison with the baseline returns Below Linear Baseline, At Linear Baseline, or Above Linear Baseline.
4. Given a zero planned amount with positive recorded actual, when pace is classified, then Over Zero Plan is returned without division.
5. Given a zero planned amount with zero or negative recorded actual, when pace is classified, then At Or Below Zero Plan is returned without division.
6. Given no plan, no active or explicit revision, a not-started period, or unavailable coherent actual evidence, when pace is requested, then the exact named unavailable state is returned and no baseline is fabricated.
7. Given any Linear Pace result, when its explanation is returned, then it states the elapsed-day fraction, formula version, exact inputs, current-day incompleteness possibility, and that the baseline assumes uniform calendar distribution and is not a Forecast.
8. Given daily or weekly exact metrics, when pace output is inspected, then no separate daily or weekly sub-budget is invented from the monthly plan.

### FR-INSIGHTS-TRUSTED-ANALYSIS-WINDOWS: Apply trusted calendar Analysis Windows

- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Define one unambiguous operation-start As-of Instant and fixed Africa/Johannesburg calendar windows over LEDGER Effective Dates. Goal trace: G1 and G2.
- **Rationale:** Implicit clocks, locale weeks, rolling ranges, and historical-state claims can reverse the meaning of pace and comparison results.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given any report generation, when processing starts, then one trusted TimeProvider instant is frozen before producer reads and returned as the As-of Instant in Source Provenance.
2. Given any As-of Instant, when its local date is derived, then the fixed IANA timezone Africa/Johannesburg is used and host locale is ignored.
3. Given a day window, when its bounds are returned, then it contains the single As-of local calendar date intersected with the selected Budget Period.
4. Given a week window, when its bounds are returned, then it begins Monday, ends Sunday, and is clipped to the selected Budget Period.
5. Given a month-to-date window, when its bounds are returned, then it begins on the Budget Period start and ends on the earlier of the As-of local date or period end, inclusive.
6. Given a Budget Period completed before the As-of local date, when the month window is returned, then the full Budget Period is selected.
7. Given a Budget Period starting after the As-of local date, when elapsed-time metrics are requested, then Not Started is returned and no zero-spend or pace interpretation is fabricated.
8. Given a rolling-day or rolling-week policy request, when it is validated, then Unsupported Analysis Window is returned before financial processing.
9. Given transaction membership in any window, when actuals are grouped, then LEDGER Effective Date controls membership and posting time or invocation time does not move a transaction between dates.
10. Given a historical Budget Period, when a report is generated now, then the report states that current corrected producer evidence was used and does not claim to reconstruct the source state that existed at a past As-of Instant.

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Analysis Window** | A versioned timezone-aware interval definition used to group or compare evidence, such as an explicit calendar day, calendar week, month-to-date, or prior comparable window. Avoid: period or range without the named policy and bounds. |
| **Anomaly** | An evidence-backed deviation from a named historical or plan-relative baseline that crosses a versioned rule; it is never inferred from an implicit or incomparable baseline. Avoid: unusual or suspicious without the cited baseline and threshold. |
| **As-of Instant** | The explicit trusted timestamp frozen before source reads that bounds elapsed-period time and the report request. Avoid: now or today without the frozen timestamp and timezone. |
| **Attention Item** | A bounded deterministically ordered report item created when one cited supported metric or signal crosses an explicit threshold and severity rule; it conveys evidence, not prescribed action. Avoid: alert, notification, or recommendation. |
| **Deletion Event** | A metadata-only attributable record that one retained Report Snapshot payload was explicitly removed, including stable identity, actor, reason, trusted time, idempotency outcome, and dependency result but no financial values or report content. Avoid: tombstone when it could imply the payload is still retained. |
| **Evidence Sufficiency** | A versioned rule deciding whether coverage, horizon, completeness, compatibility, and comparability are adequate to emit a metric or signal rather than an explicit insufficient-evidence state. Avoid: confidence when no probabilistic confidence is calculated. |
| **Forecast** | A bounded estimate of an end-of-period exact value produced only under a named versioned formula and satisfied Evidence Sufficiency rule, with inputs and limitations exposed. Avoid: prediction or expected spend without those qualifiers. |
| **Insight Report** | One deterministic analytical result for an explicit Budget Period, As-of Instant, Analysis Window set, coherent source evidence, and formula/threshold versions; it contains exact metrics, evidence states, signals, Attention Items, and Source Provenance. Avoid: dashboard or report when the immutable analytical result is meant. |
| **Linear Pace Baseline** | The exact rational share of a monthly planned amount through the As-of local date: planned minor units multiplied by elapsed calendar days and divided by total Budget Period days. It is a transparent comparison baseline, not a prediction, recommendation, daily sub-budget, or claim that spending is uniformly distributed. |
| **Pace Signal** | A deterministic plan-relative state about exact actual spend through the As-of Instant under a named formula and Evidence Sufficiency result; it is not a recommendation or enforcement action. Avoid: on track without cited evidence. |
| **Public INSIGHTS Operation** | One versioned discoverable non-interactive operation using the canonical INSIGHTS verb vocabulary with explicit request, result, error, exit, mutability, authority, idempotency, compatibility, and limit metadata. Avoid: endpoint or command when the transport-neutral contract is meant. |
| **Report Snapshot** | An immutable owner-only retained Insight Report preserving exactly what was generated and shown with stable identity, content fingerprint, Source Provenance, and creation evidence. Avoid: cache, current report, or saved view. |
| **Restatement** | A new Insight Report explicitly linked to an earlier Report Snapshot and generated from later corrected source evidence or selected policy versions; it never overwrites the original. Avoid: edit, refresh, or replacement. |
| **Source Provenance** | The complete identities and versions needed to explain and reproduce a report, including upstream contract and snapshot identities, Budget Plan Revision, Analysis Windows, As-of Instant, formula, thresholds, sufficiency rules, and content fingerprints. Avoid: provenance that names only creation time or module version. |
| **Trend** | A deterministic comparison of comparable metrics across two or more explicit Analysis Windows and stable identities, or an unavailable state when comparability is not proven. Avoid: trend based only on display-name similarity or one uncited change. |
