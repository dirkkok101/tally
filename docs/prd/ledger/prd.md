## Problem Statement

Dirk cannot trust personal-spend actuals across three accounts at two banks when one economic event may be captured from a daily notification and later appear again on a monthly statement, evidence cannot be linked or reconciled without duplication, and account, payment instrument, cardholder, spend pool, and category are conflated. Root cause: Tally lacks a provider-neutral canonical ledger with multi-evidence lineage, attributable reconciliation decisions, and independent attribution dimensions. Desired journey: the owner or Hermes records one event with generic evidence, INGEST later reconciles statement evidence match-first, ambiguous or absent cases remain reviewable, the owner corrects attribution explicitly, and BUDGET or INSIGHTS consumes exact pool/category results through public contracts.

## Personas

### P1: Personal Finance Owner

- **Role:** Sole owner and decision-maker for household finances
- **Goals:** When reviewing or planning personal spending, I want one trustworthy view across all owned accounts, evidence sources, spend pools, and categories so I can delegate maintenance safely and know why each transaction is present.
- **Pain Points:** Daily notifications and later statements can describe the same economic event; transactions are fragmented across three accounts at two banks; account, cardholder, payment instrument, pool, and category can be conflated; manual consolidation has weak provenance and uncertain duplicate handling.

- **Success Looks Like:** The owner can delegate daily capture and monthly reconciliation, review every ambiguous or missing match, and retrieve exact pool/category totals whose evidence and decision history are inspectable.
- **Tech Level:** high

| C12 | Verified backup restore and migration: Backups are consistent, version-identifiable, restricted, and contain complete Durable Ledger State, including evidence, reconciliation, attribution, pools, and idempotency outcomes. | C13 | No custom cryptography: Encryption at rest, if required beyond host storage encryption, must use a maintained storage/cryptography provider and platform-protected key handling. | C14 | Stable process stream contract: Structured command results are emitted on stdout, diagnostics on stderr, and expected failures carry stable module-scoped error codes plus documented non-zero exits. | C15 | Snapshot-consistent paginated queries: A multi-page query is bound to the transaction membership, reconciliation/evidence state, attribution, and exact totals established when its first page is created. Later writes cannot change remaining pages; an unavailable snapshot fails explicitly rather than switching to live state. | technical |
| C16 | Attributable transaction void and supersession: Erroneous Canonical Transaction facts are never edited or silently deleted. An explicit reasoned void or supersession preserves the original, creates a separate replacement when needed, retires incompatible active relationships atomically, and never silently moves evidence, Reconciliation Decision, or attribution to the replacement. Default actuals count only the valid outcome; history exposes the full chain. | business |
| C17 | Attributable active relationship lifecycle: Transfer and refund or reversal links have attributable active and retired history with exclusive roles and amount conservation. Only active facts and relationships affect actuals. A linked refund offsets the original transaction's current category and Spend Pool in the refund's own Effective Date period. Void, supersession, revocation, or replacement revalidates and updates category and pool actuals atomically. | business |
| C18 | Provider-neutral evidence boundary: Evidence Kind are generic, including agent capture, statement row, receipt, external document, and owner assertion. Ledger stores only stable evidence identity, privacy-conscious metadata, content or logical fingerprints where needed, Opaque External Reference, and link/decision history. It never stores or interprets mailbox, MIME, messaging, provider cursor, recipient, credential, or delivery payloads. | technical |
| C19 | Observable reconciliation lifecycle: Ledger must represent recorded but unreconciled, statement reconciled with confirmed-existing or corrected-from-statement detail, statement-only, recorded absent from an approved statement scope, ambiguous match, explicitly owner-confirmed match, and Reconciliation Exception outcomes. Current state may be derived, but every transition, authoritative replacement, scope, policy or owner actor, reason, carry-forward event, and superseded decision remains inspectable. | business |
| C20 | Deterministic reconciliation safety: An approved statement row is authoritative over earlier agent-capture evidence for current canonical facts, but automatic reconciliation or correction is permitted only when documented compatible evidence produces one strong deterministic candidate. Zero candidates may create one Statement-only Transaction through an approved INGEST request; multiple or conflicting candidates remain unapplied. Owner-confirmed match or correction requires explicit intent, reason, and evidence. No active statement evidence may confirm more than one active transaction. | business |
| C21 | Independent financial attribution dimensions: Owned account, Payment Instrument, cardholder, Spend Pool, and Spend Category are distinct stable dimensions. Ledger must not infer one solely from another or from provider wording. Optional or unknown attribution remains explicit; every assignment and correction is attributable and historical references survive archival. | business |
| C22 | Exact pool and category actual conservation: Every active spend transaction is either assigned to one active Spend Pool or exposed in an explicit unassigned-pool bucket, and to zero or one direct Spend Category independently. Transfers contribute zero External Spend to all pools and categories. Category exact and subtree roll-ups use the current acyclic hierarchy and never duplicate a transaction. Linked refunds and reversals offset the original current pool/category in their own Effective Date period. Corrections, statement-authoritative replacements, hierarchy moves, and relationship changes update exact current actuals atomically without overwriting history. | business |
| C23 | Reconciliation correction composition: Evidence links and Reconciliation Decision are corrected only by attributable revoke, reject, replacement, or statement-authoritative correction history. Ordinary void or supersession never silently moves evidence, confirmation, or dimensions. A statement-authoritative correction may create one replacement and explicit carry-forward assignment events atomically under its decision; the prior fact and links remain historical, and active actuals count only the replacement. | business |

### OQ-LEDGER-9: Should every write command support both explicit CLI options and JSON standard input, and how are command schemas versioned?

- **Context:** The CLI must remain pleasant for direct use while being deterministic and stable for agents and bundled skills.
- **Decision:** Every structured Ledger write accepts JSON standard input and may offer explicit options for small scalar fields. Every command exposes a versioned machine-readable contract; stdout is structured result data, stderr is diagnostics, and stable non-zero exits accompany structured expected failures.

### OQ-LEDGER-8: Which maintained embedded storage, host encryption, and key-protection combination satisfies C4/C11-C13 without custom cryptography?

- **Context:** Low-confidence feasibility item: run /lex:spike before planning to test C4/C11-C13 plus UC-LEDGER-007/012 outcomes—owner-only permissions, encrypted-at-rest posture, consistent backup, verification, restore, atomic migration/upgrade, and recovery-key behavior. PRD states outcomes without selecting the provider.
- **Decision:** Use .NET 10 Native AOT with raw Microsoft.Data.Sqlite and the maintained bundled SQLite library; enforce 0700/0600 private artifacts, integrity verification, separate store generations, and atomic CURRENT-pointer activation. Stock SQLite does not encrypt files, so a supported deployment must place the data root on host full-disk/volume encryption with OS-managed keys. No custom cryptography. The spike preserved exact totals, relationships, idempotency, and schema state across backup, separate restore, migration, and activation; the current guest-visible ext4 path does not itself prove host encryption.

### OQ-LEDGER-7: Which transaction fields remain immutable canonical facts, and which evidence, reconciliation, attribution, or correction changes require attributable history?

- **Context:** Notification capture, statement reconciliation, owner clarification, classification, Pool Assignment, and lifecycle correction require a common provider-neutral lineage model without one mutable provenance field.
- **Decision:** Account, original description, exact signed amount, currency, and supplied bank dates are immutable Canonical Transaction facts. Evidence is stored as separate append-only Evidence Record and links, not as one immutable sourceReference or provenance slot. Reconciliation, category, Spend Pool, Payment Instrument/cardholder, transfer/refund, void, and supersession changes preserve attributable history; invalid facts or decisions are revoked, superseded, or replaced rather than edited in place.

### OQ-LEDGER-6: Validate flat, archivable categories with one active category allocation per transaction for the first release.

- **Context:** Resolved from direct owner policy. A5 is validated and A10 is invalidated. The PRD and design must replace flat-catalogue assumptions with an acyclic hierarchy and preserve one active Category Assignment per transaction.
- **Decision:** Owner decision on 2026-07-21: Spend Category use a hierarchy, and each transaction links to at most one active category. Flat categories are rejected; split category allocation is not required. The technical design owns hierarchy integrity and roll-up semantics, while BUDGET separately decides whether plans reference category nodes.

### OQ-LEDGER-5: Validate the proposed transfer, refund/reversal, and cash-withdrawal spend policies with representative transactions.

- **Context:** Resolved from direct owner policy. The former bundled cash-withdrawal ambiguity moved to OQ-LEDGER-16 so transfer implementation is no longer blocked by an unrelated cash-account choice.
- **Decision:** Owner decision on 2026-07-21: movements between owned accounts are transfers and contribute zero External Spend or Budget Actual while both bank-account legs remain ordinary queryable account movements. Normal banking semantics apply to separately recorded bank fees as spend and linked refunds or reversals as offsets to the original spend. Cash-withdrawal treatment is deliberately separated into OQ-LEDGER-16.

### OQ-LEDGER-16: Should a cash withdrawal count as External Spend immediately, or as a transfer to a tracked cash account until later cash spending is recorded?

- **Context:** Resolved by explicit owner approval on 2026-07-21. V1 has no tracked-cash account: a cash withdrawal is recognized as spend when it leaves the bank account, preventing later unrecorded cash use from understating actuals.
- **Decision:** Owner decision on 2026-07-21: a cash withdrawal is immediate External Spend and Budget Actual in its Effective Date period; it is not an owned-account transfer because v1 has no tracked-cash account. Any separately recorded withdrawal fee remains ordinary spend. A linked reversal or refund offsets the withdrawal under the normal relationship rules. Later use of the same withdrawn cash is not recorded again as additional spend, preventing double counting.

### OQ-LEDGER-15: Does one active Spend Pool per transaction cover company-paid and personal-after-tax examples, including corrections, refunds, reversals, and mixed purchases?

- **Context:** Resolved by explicit owner approval on 2026-07-21. V1 uses one active Spend Pool Assignment or explicit unassigned state per transaction. Mixed purchases do not create split-pool allocations; they remain unassigned until the owner chooses one pool or a later design explicitly adds splitting.
- **Decision:** Owner decision on 2026-07-21: each active spend transaction has at most one active Spend Pool Assignment, independent of account, Payment Instrument, cardholder, and category, or remains explicitly unassigned. Corrections append history and replace the active assignment. Refunds and reversals follow the original transaction's current pool, transfer principal contributes zero spend, and mixed purchases remain unassigned until assigned wholly to one pool. Split-pool allocation is out of scope for v1.

### OQ-LEDGER-14: Which privacy-safe stable Payment Instrument and cardholder identifiers are available across the initial accounts and notification or statement evidence?

- **Context:** Validate against private fixtures before design. Full card or account numbers are prohibited; missing attribution must remain explicit, and Hermes may map provider wording only to stable Tally identifiers.
- **Decision:** Use stable local Tally ULIDs as authoritative Payment Instrument and cardholder identities, owner-managed labels, optional masked suffixes, and explicit unknown states. Full provider identifiers are prohibited; Hermes may map provider wording to these IDs. Private fixtures still validate mappings before production entry.

### OQ-LEDGER-13: Which provider-neutral compatibility fields, tolerances, and conflict rules are sufficient for an automatic deterministic match between agent-capture and statement-row evidence?

- **Context:** Resolved from the owner-approved conservative policy on 2026-07-22. The redacted symbolic matrix at docs/validation/ledger/oq-13-reconciliation-policy.md records outcomes without retaining or inferring private statement data.
- **Decision:** ReconciliationPolicyV1 automatically matches only when approved statement-row evidence has exact owned account identity, ZAR currency, signed Int64 minor-unit amount, and Effective Date with zero-day tolerance; exactly one active recorded-but-unreconciled compatible candidate and zero guard candidates survive; and neither evidence nor candidate has a conflicting active confirmation or decision. The deterministic reason is exact_unique_candidate, the decision records the four-field basis and policy version, and replay is idempotent. Posting date, payment attribution, description, provider data, Evidence Kind, and candidate order are not compatibility fields or tie-breakers. Differing facts, shifted dates, multiple or guard candidates, conflicts, already-reconciled candidates, and stale or unsupported policy are review-required and apply no automatic financial effect. The automatic correction subset is empty. Zero-candidate statement-only creation and statement-authoritative correction remain explicit owner-approved dispositions with attributable append-only history.

### OQ-LEDGER-12: Can an existing finance CLI satisfy every current LEDGER goal G1-G12 through public commands and a thin Tally adapter?

- **Decision:** hledger remains useful analogous prior art but a thin adapter does not provide Tally's provider-neutral Evidence Record, append-only Reconciliation Decision and exceptions, independent attribution dimensions, uniform structured operation contracts, or complete verified recovery semantics. Supplying G1-G12 would require a substantial Tally domain layer rather than a thin adapter, so the custom Ledger kill criterion is not triggered.

### OQ-LEDGER-11: Is a web interface likely within the first delivery horizon, or can an agent or WhatsApp-style adapter remain the only human interaction path?

- **Context:** A web selection mandates PostgreSQL, Entity Framework Core, latest-stable .NET and Angular, and NxGN UI; resolving timing avoids an accidental embedded-to-service rewrite.
- **Decision:** ADR-CORE-0030 resolves the boundary: Tally remains a local provider-neutral CLI, Hermes owns external orchestration and delivery, and CHANNELS is not a Tally implementation module.

### OQ-LEDGER-10: Which external callers receive bundled skills or contract guidance, and what compatibility rule binds each caller to a Tally contract version?

- **Context:** Public Ledger operations must remain caller-neutral. Codex, Claude Code, direct owner use, and Hermes may consume the same versioned command registry without private storage or provider-specific fields.
- **Decision:** The executable exposes one provider-neutral versioned operation registry and schemas. Codex and Claude Code may receive version-matched convenience skills; Hermes consumes the same released contract as an External Orchestrator. Every caller fails compatibility before financial mutation and no caller-specific transport behavior enters LEDGER semantics.

### UC-LEDGER-001: Maintain an Owned Bank Account

- **Primary Actor:** P1
- **Trigger:** The owner wants to register, rename, inspect, or retire one of the three owned bank accounts.
- **Depth Tier:** 2
- **Status:** draft

**Preconditions:**

**Scenario:**

1. The owner delegates the account action to an AI agent. 2. The agent discovers the account command schema. 3. **Postconditions:**

The owned account has one stable reference; historical transaction references survive rename or archival; archived accounts cannot receive new transactions by default.

**Failure Paths:**

- 3a. Required identifying data is absent -> reject with field-level structured errors
- 4a. The masked identifier conflicts with another active account -> reject without mutation
- 4b. Credential-like data is supplied -> reject the unsupported secret-bearing input
- 5a. A referenced account is requested for deletion -> archive it or reject physical deletion

### UC-LEDGER-002: Record a Canonical Transaction

- **Primary Actor:** P1
- **Trigger:** The owner or external orchestrator has one provider-neutral financial event to record from an agent capture, statement row, receipt, external document, or owner assertion.
- **Depth Tier:** 3
- **Status:** draft

**Preconditions:**

The target owned account exists and is active; input follows a discoverable contract; provider parsing occurred outside LEDGER; the request includes an Idempotency Identity and privacy-safe initial evidence.

**Scenario:**

1. The caller supplies exact Canonical Transaction facts plus a generic Evidence Kind, Opaque External Reference, privacy-safe metadata, and Idempotency Identity. 2. Tally validates financial semantics, account state, evidence boundary, and attribution references. 3. Tally checks logical evidence and request replay before creating anything. 4. Tally atomically records at most one Canonical Transaction and links the initial evidence without embedding provider payloads. 5. Tally returns the transaction, evidence identity, current Reconciliation State, and contract version. 6. A later query round-trips every accepted fact and link exactly.

**Postconditions:**

Exactly one Canonical Transaction and one logical initial evidence effect exist; provider payloads are absent; same replay returns the original outcome.

**Failure Paths:**

- 1a. Transaction, evidence, attribution, or idempotency input is invalid -> reject with structured errors
- 2a. Account is absent or archived -> reject without mutation
- 2b. Evidence contains provider payload or prohibited sensitive identifiers -> reject without retention
- 3a. The request or evidence already exists identically -> return the existing result with no duplicate effect
- 3b. The key or evidence identity conflicts with existing facts or another active transaction -> return a stable conflict and preserve the original
- 4a. Storage fails or is interrupted -> commit transaction plus evidence link together or leave neither

### UC-LEDGER-004: Confirm an owned-account transfer

- **Primary Actor:** P1
- **Trigger:** Two canonical transactions appear to be the legs of a transfer between accounts owned by the owner.
- **Depth Tier:** 3
- **Status:** draft

### FR-LEDGER-ACCOUNT-MAINTENANCE: Maintain Owned Bank Account

- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Create and maintain stable local records for owned asset and liability accounts using masked identity fields only. Implements G1 and UC-LEDGER-001.
- **Rationale:** All downstream transaction facts need an unambiguous owned-account identity without introducing banking access or credential storage.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given a valid unique institution name, owner label, supported asset or liability account type, ZAR currency, display label, and masked distinguishing identifier, when the owner creates an account, then the Ledger returns one active account with a stable identifier and stores no online-banking credential or full account number.
2. Given an existing account, when the owner renames its display label, then its stable identifier and previously recorded transaction references remain unchanged.
3. Given an account referenced by transactions, when the owner archives it, then its history remains queryable and the Ledger rejects new transactions against it with a stable validation error.
4. Given active and archived accounts, when the owner lists accounts with an explicit lifecycle-state filter, then each matching account is returned once with its stable identifier and masked identity fields.
5. Given an owned account, Payment Instrument, cardholders, Spend Pool, and categories, when account maintenance occurs, then no account value silently creates, selects, or changes another dimension.

### FR-LEDGER-ACTUALS-QUERY: Query exact Ledger actuals

- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Return exact transaction membership and named aggregates across category, pool, attribution, evidence, and reconciliation dimensions for downstream BUDGET and INSIGHTS consumers. Implements G6, G10-G12, and UC-LEDGER-005.
- **Rationale:** Analysis is trustworthy only when every total can be traced to the exact selected transactions under explicit active-state semantics.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given explicit filters for account, inclusive date range, category, Spend Pool, Payment Instrument, cardholder, Evidence Kind, Reconciliation State, transfer state, refund state, categorization state, Pool Assignment state, and transaction lifecycle state, when actuals are queried, then only transactions matching every supplied filter are returned.
2. Given no lifecycle-state override, when actuals are queried, then each active economic event contributes through exactly one current Canonical Transaction; voided or superseded facts and retired relationships or assignments do not affect default totals.
3. Given any selected transaction set, when Net Account Movement is returned, then it equals the exact base-10 sum of each selected active transaction's Signed Account Amount, with inflows positive and outflows negative.
4. Given a selected transaction set, when External Spend is returned, then it adds positive magnitudes of qualifying active outflows, subtracts active linked refund or reversal magnitudes in their own Effective Date periods, excludes active owned-transfer principal and unrelated inflows, includes separately recorded fees, and never silently chooses a cash-withdrawal policy while OQ-LEDGER-16 is unresolved.
5. Given category grouping with scope exact or subtree, when Budget Actual is returned, then each qualifying active transaction contributes once to its direct category or uncategorized bucket and, for subtree scope, once to each requested ancestor roll-up; pool and pool-by-category totals reconcile exactly without hierarchy double counting.
6. Given an active linked refund or reversal, when grouped actuals are queried, then it offsets the original transaction's current pool and category, including explicit unassigned buckets, without inheriting account, instrument, or cardholder as a pool rule.
7. Given no matching transactions, when actuals are queried, then the Ledger returns a successful empty collection and zero exact totals rather than a not-found error.
8. Given unchanged Ledger state and identical filters, when the query is repeated, then membership, ordering, reconciliation and attribution state, and exact aggregates are identical.
9. Given an approved statement-authoritative correction changes account, amount, or Effective Date, when current actuals are queried, then the superseded notification-derived fact contributes zero and the active statement-derived replacement contributes exactly once under its explicitly carried-forward or corrected dimensions.

Given an active account, valid original description, exact base-10 signed amount, ZAR currency, transaction date, allowlisted initial evidence metadata, and Idempotency Identity, when recording succeeds, then one stable Canonical Transaction and one linked Evidence Record are created atomically without precision loss.
2. Given a transaction with a distinct supplied posting date, when it is recorded, then both transaction date and posting date round-trip without substituting one for the other.
3. Given any missing required fact, unsupported currency, zero amount, malformed date, archived account, unsupported Evidence Kind, or forbidden provider payload, when recording is attempted, then a stable validation error is returned and neither transaction nor evidence is created.
4. Given a recorded transaction, when it is read later, then its account identity, original description, signed amount, currency, supplied dates, stable identifier, evidence links, current Reconciliation State, and explicit unknown attribution states round-trip without silently inferring category, pool, instrument, or cardholder.
5. Given a movement on an active owned asset or liability account, when its signed amount is recorded, then a positive value means the owner's economic position improved and a negative value means it worsened: asset increases and liability reductions are positive; asset decreases and liability increases are negative.
6. Given the same logical initial evidence and semantically identical Canonical Transaction facts are submitted through another request identity, when recording is attempted, then the original transaction and evidence result is returned and no duplicate effect is created.
7. Given the same logical initial evidence is submitted with materially different Canonical Transaction facts, when recording is attempted, then a stable conflict is returned and the existing transaction and evidence remain unchanged.
8. Given a Canonical Transaction created through the ordinary recording operation, when its initial state is returned, then its Reconciliation State is recorded-but-unreconciled until a valid deterministic or owner-approved Reconciliation Decision changes it.

### FR-LEDGER-TRANSFER-CONFIRMATION: Confirm owned-account transfers

- **Priority:** Critical
- **Description:** Explicitly link qualifying transaction legs as a transfer between owned accounts. Implements G4 and UC-LEDGER-004 under A11.
- **Rationale:** Transfers must stay visible in account history while contributing zero External Spend.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. Given two active transactions on distinct active owned accounts with ZAR equal-and-opposite economic movements, when the owner confirms them as a transfer with a reason, then one active attributable transfer relationship is created.
2. Given an active confirmed transfer, when default External Spend actuals are queried, then the paired principal contributes exactly zero while both transaction legs remain queryable.
3. Given qualifying legs with different supplied dates, when the owner explicitly confirms the relationship, then the date difference alone does not prevent confirmation.
4. Given same-account legs, same-sign legs, unequal principal magnitudes, different currencies, inactive legs, or either leg already participating in any active transfer or refund relationship, when transfer confirmation is attempted, then the Ledger returns a stable validation error and creates no relationship.
5. Given a bank fee recorded separately from the equal-and-opposite principal legs, when the transfer is confirmed, then the fee remains an unlinked transaction and retains its own spend treatment.
6. Given a confirmed transfer whose legs have category or Spend Pool Assignment, when pool, category, or pool-by-category actuals are queried, then principal contributes exactly zero to every bucket while attribution remains queryable.

## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Archived Ledger Entity** | An account or category retained for historical references but unavailable for ordinary new assignments or transactions. Avoid: deleted, erased, inactive history. |
| **Budget Actual** | The exact External Spend restricted by current Spend Category and Spend Pool assignment. Active linked refunds use their own Effective Date and offset the original current category and pool; transfers contribute zero. Results expose uncategorized and unassigned-pool amounts separately. BUDGET compares this Ledger-owned actual with planned amounts. |
| **Canonical Transaction** | Tally's durable representation of one current economic event on one owned account. Facts are immutable; an approved statement may replace an earlier agent-capture fact through attributable supersession so exactly one version remains active while all evidence and history stay inspectable. Avoid: one active row per source observation or in-place overwrite. |
| **Cardholder Attribution** | A stable owner-managed identity indicating the person or role attributed to use of a Payment Instrument or transaction when relevant. It is optional, provider-neutral, and independently correctable. Avoid: recipient, Tally application user, bank account owner, or automatic inference from description. |
| **Category Assignment** | The attributable active or historical relationship between one Canonical Transaction and zero or one Spend Category node. It is independent from account, Payment Instrument, Cardholder Attribution, Spend Pool, and Pool Assignment; current hierarchy roll-ups derive from the category node rather than creating extra assignments. Avoid: split category allocation or using category as funding ownership. |
| **Durable Ledger State** | All authoritative state required to preserve behavior after restart or recovery: storage metadata; accounts, payment instruments, cardholders, spend pools, and categories with lifecycle history; Canonical Transactions; Evidence Records and links; Reconciliation Decisions, states, scopes, and exceptions; transaction, allocation, attribution, transfer and refund history; and idempotency outcomes. Rebuildable cursors and caches are excluded. |
| **Effective Date** | The explicit calendar date used to include a Canonical Transaction in period queries under the accepted date policy. Avoid: import timestamp, current time, or a fabricated timezone conversion. |
| **Evidence Kind** | A closed provider-neutral purpose classification such as agent capture, statement row, receipt, external document, or owner assertion. It describes what the evidence represents, not which transport supplied it. Avoid: AgentMail, WhatsApp, bank brand, MIME type, or delivery channel as a domain kind. |
| **Evidence Record** | A durable provider-neutral observation supporting or questioning one economic event, with a stable identity, Evidence Kind, privacy-conscious metadata, opaque external reference, and replay identity. It may be unlinked while ambiguous and one Canonical Transaction may have many. Avoid: raw email, MIME part, statement document payload, or synonym for Canonical Transaction. |
| **External Orchestrator** | A local automation system that invokes versioned Tally public commands under the owner OS identity and consumes structured results. Hermes is the current deployment and owns provider transport, conversation, schedules, recipients, rendering, delivery, and retries. It is a supporting actor, not a human persona. Avoid: treating Hermes, AgentMail, WhatsApp, or delivery behavior as LEDGER semantics. |
| **External Spend** | An exact net-spend amount for a selected set: add the positive magnitudes of active outflows in the set that are not principal legs of active owned-account transfers, then subtract the magnitudes of active linked refund/reversal credits in the set. Separately recorded fees and unlinked cash withdrawals remain included; unrelated inflows are excluded. A period can be negative when in-period refunds exceed in-period outflows. Avoid: raw signed account movement or every negative transaction. |
| **Idempotency Identity** | A caller-visible stable identity for one intended public Ledger mutation. Same-operation, same-contract, semantically identical reuse yields the original result; reuse for another operation/version or a materially different request fails without mutation. Validation failures do not consume it. Avoid: fuzzy duplicate key, bank-description match, or database row ID. |
| **Net Account Movement** | The exact base-10 sum of Signed Account Amount values for selected Canonical Transactions. Positive means net improvement and negative means net deterioration in the owner economic position. It is distinct from External Spend and Budget Actual, which transform qualifying outflows to positive magnitudes, subtract linked refund magnitudes, and exclude unrelated inflows and owned-transfer principal. |
| **Opaque External Reference** | A caller-supplied privacy-safe token used to correlate an Evidence Record with state outside Tally. Ledger compares it only under documented identity rules and does not parse provider semantics from it. Avoid: full card or account number, mailbox payload, recipient address, or provider cursor. |
| **Owned Bank Account** | A local record representing one of Dirk's accounts at a financial institution, identified without storing login credentials or an unmasked secret account number. |
| **Payment Instrument** | A stable privacy-safe identity for a card or other means used to execute a transaction, optionally associated with an owned account and Cardholder Attribution. It may affect matching or reporting but is not the account, cardholder, spend pool, or category. Avoid: full card number, provider-specific wording, or account alias. |
| **Pool Assignment** | The attributable current or historical relationship between one Canonical Transaction and one Spend Pool, or an explicit unassigned state. Corrections append history; transfers contribute zero and linked refunds follow the original current pool. Avoid: deriving pool silently from account, instrument, cardholder, merchant, or category. |
| **Reconciliation Decision** | An append-only attributable determination that statement evidence confirms an existing transaction, authoritatively corrects it through replacement, creates a statement-only outcome, remains ambiguous, is owner-confirmed or rejected, or supersedes an earlier decision. It records deterministic policy or explicit owner authority, reason, statement basis, and affected history. Avoid: mutable matched flags, row overwrite, or silent fuzzy guesses. |
| **Reconciliation Exception** | A structured review-needed outcome such as conflicting evidence, invalid candidate, recorded transaction absent from a completed statement scope, stale decision, or lifecycle conflict. It preserves scope and reason and never authorizes a guessed match. Avoid: generic error with no recoverable context. |
| **Reconciliation State** | The current observable outcome derived from reconciliation history: recorded unreconciled, statement reconciled, statement-only, recorded absent from an approved statement scope, ambiguous match, explicitly owner-confirmed match, or reconciliation exception. Avoid: transport acknowledgement, statement import status, or unscoped missing flag. |
| **Signed Account Amount** | The exact base-10 owner economic-position delta for an Owned Bank Account. Positive means an asset increases or a liability owed decreases; negative means an asset decreases or a liability owed increases. Avoid: unsigned spend, raw display debit/credit label, or binary floating-point value. |
| **Source Provenance** | Provider-neutral lineage explaining which Evidence Records and Reconciliation Decisions support a Canonical Transaction and how they changed over time. It is an assembled history, not one transport-specific source field. Avoid: mailbox message ID as domain identity, MIME payload, single provenance slot, or import method as proof of uniqueness. |
| **Spend Category** | A stable user-managed classification node in LEDGER's acyclic category hierarchy. A transaction has zero or one direct Category Assignment; exact and subtree queries roll that assignment through current ancestry without double counting. BUDGET may reference the stable node but owns plan semantics. |
| **Spend Pool** | A stable owner-managed funding or responsibility dimension used to separate actual spending, including company-paid discretionary or personal-expense spending from personal after-tax spending. It is independent from account, Payment Instrument, Cardholder Attribution, and Spend Category. Avoid: budget amount, bank account, category group, or cardholder. |
| **Statement-only Transaction** | A Canonical Transaction created from an approved statement row after no compatible prior transaction exists. It has statement evidence but no earlier notification or owner-capture evidence at creation. Avoid: duplicate import row or proof that no other evidence can arrive later. |
| **Transfer Pair** | Exactly two active equal-and-opposite principal transactions on distinct owned accounts, each participating in that one active transfer and in no active refund relationship. Both facts remain queryable while their principal contributes zero External Spend; separately recorded fees remain outside the pair. |
| **Uncategorized Spend** | A transaction currently included in spend but not assigned to a spend category; it remains visible and reportable rather than being silently forced into a category. |
| **Verified Ledger Backup** | A point-in-time, version-identifiable, access-restricted artifact containing the complete Durable Ledger State, whose compatibility, integrity, per-entity counts, relationship/cardinality rules, idempotency outcomes, and exact derived totals can be checked independently without mutating the live Ledger. Avoid: unverified file copy or transaction-only export report. |
